Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

Imran Social Media Bot is a social media management application designed to help manage Instagram content, comments, and messages.

Information We Access

With your permission, the application may access information provided through the Instagram API, including basic Instagram account information, media, comments, and messages required to provide the application's features.

How We Use Information

Information accessed through the Instagram API is used only to provide the requested social media management features, such as managing content, processing comments, and handling messages.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information is not shared with third parties except when required to operate the service or comply with applicable law.

Data Retention and Deletion

Users may request deletion of data associated with the application. Instructions for requesting deletion are provided on our Data Deletion page.

Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information processed by the application. Access credentials and API tokens are kept confidential and are not intentionally made public.
