User Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: August 22, 2026

Users of Imran Social Media Bot may request deletion of data associated with their Instagram account.

How to Request Data Deletion

To request deletion of your data, send an email to imran-ichiigo@hotmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request".

Please include the Instagram username associated with your request so that the relevant data can be identified.

Disconnecting the Application

You may also remove the application's access from your Instagram account through the Apps and Websites section of your Instagram settings.

Processing Requests

Valid deletion requests will be processed as soon as reasonably possible. Data that must be retained for legal or security purposes may be retained only as required by applicable law.

Contact

For questions regarding data deletion, contact: imran-ichiigo@hotmail.com
